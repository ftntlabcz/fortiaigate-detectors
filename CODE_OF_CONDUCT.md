# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

We want this to be a friendly place to improve a shared reference. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests or discussions, is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent, whatever someone's background or level of experience,
- give and accept feedback on the content, not the person,
- stay on topic and back claims about laws and standards with sources,
- respect people's privacy and never share someone's personal information without consent.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks, sexualized content, and any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces on GitHub and whenever someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately through the [ftntlabcz organization on GitHub](https://github.com/ftntlabcz). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently block contributors who do so repeatedly or seriously.
